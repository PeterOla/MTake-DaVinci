# A second story, taken to the camera decision

A fresh agent received the skill and a different brief. Plan a 35-second vertical film about why a bicycle reflector lights up when a headlight catches it. It produced [this single camera sheet](camera.png) before detailed drawing. This is a planning example. The 0.85 m move and 0.70 m camera height are staging choices to test on a real specimen.

| Time | Cause and visible result | Narrator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | On a locked exposure a headlight switches on. The rear reflector glows above the bicycle tyre. Give the audience a beat to find it. | “Watch the red patch just above the tyre.” |
| 5–11 s | Keep the lamp fixed. Slide the camera sideways. A diffuse strip below the reflector remains illuminated while the reflector looks dimmer. | “The lamp is still on. Where did the red go?” |
| 11–17 s | Keep the housing visible as one cell opens in an enlarged illustration beside it. | “Inside this reflector, three tiny faces meet at right angles.” |
| 17–23 s | Trace one ray through all three faces. Let the returning direction read. | “Light bounces off all three and heads back toward the headlight.” |
| 23–29 s | The camera returns along the same path. The strip stays in the frame. | “Watch the tape as we come back.” |
| 29–35 s | Near the headlight, the reflector brightens again. Hold the opening composition for the final three seconds. | “Beside the headlight, your eyes catch some of that returning light.” |

The proposed direction relies on a **prismatic** reflector. A new production needs a real specimen photo and its optical construction. Test the view at fixed exposure and adjust the lateral move until a viewer can compare the reflector with the matte strip. Confirm the voice and final hold on a scratch read. A director still needs to approve this move to commission the film. [3M on retroreflection](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/295767O/reflectivity-flyer.pdf)

The standalone trial exposed a sequencing problem. An early version asked for shot files before an artist could check the artwork. The art review now runs on the registered subjects and camera sheet before the shot list exists. Another issue left links in the generated film's production notes pointing back into the skill. The example now writes its own record with working local links.
