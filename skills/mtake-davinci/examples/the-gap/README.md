# The Gap

A nine-second, 540 × 960, 30 fps original short. Four dominoes fall across a workbench. One stays standing. The audience gets to see why before the second spoken line. This is a small reproducible test of the method. A new film gets its own story, source material, direction and editable shots.

## Production record

| Frames (zero-based) | Picture and viewer task | Voice / sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–22 | Start close to a row. The first tile tips from its lower edge. Its neighbour is already visible. | A low note and a short brush introduce movement. |
| 23, 32, 41, 58 | Three contacts pass along the row. The fourth tile reaches the table. The camera pulls out, revealing the longer interval. | Four original pitched accents and four separate dry wooden transients. Voice stays clear of the whole cluster. |
| 70–86 | The fifth tile is fully visible and upright. Hold the evidence while the last resonance dies. | No speech. |
| 87–143 | Keep the standing tile and fallen row together. | “The last one is still standing.” |
| 144–187 | Move toward the interval. A fine bracket measures only the space between the reachable edge and the final tile. | Allow time to notice what was missed. |
| 188–269 | Hold on both ends of the interval. | “One gap broke the chain.” One quiet musical tail ends the scene. |

The camera decision belongs to this authored example. For your own film, use your director's approval or recorded delegation. The [single movement sheet](camera.svg) shows the route. The editable mesh has real face depth, rounded corners, a centre division and inset-looking pips drawn from [Roland Scheicher's domino photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Domino.JPG). The numerical dimensions, toppling and new dark finish are an original construction. The full photograph can be seen in [the reference](reference.jpg). See [credits and licences](../../CREDITS.md).

Each tile shares [domino.obj](domino.obj), generated by [make_tile.py](make_tile.py). Import the OBJ in Blender for mesh editing. The sample renderer reads these vertices and face groups directly. The fixed standing tile can be switched off at its source while keeping the other four, lighting and camera identical. The review board uses that same camera for neutral and ink. The executable picture and sound are in the skill's scripts directory.

## Rebuild or reuse

From the repository root run:

~~~sh
python skills/mtake-davinci/scripts/mtake.py demo build/the-gap
~~~

Open the resulting the-gap.mp4. The output includes aligned stereo WAV stems, original caption timings as SRT, the encoded silent shot, the model, photo, model-on and model-off frames, a photo/neutral/linework comparison and an export measurement report. View frame 103 at phone size and look for the rightmost tile. Listen to the contacts without the voice, then play the whole film at 1×. For a real commissioned picture, have a fresh viewer describe why the fifth tile stands.

The two supplied spoken takes were generated for this example. You can replace them with a recorded performer. Re-time the phrase and accessibility captions to the actual new take. To reuse the editor on a different story, supply new shots and WAVs through the [new film instructions](../../references/tools.md).
