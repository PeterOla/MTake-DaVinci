# MTake DaVinci

Give one viewer something to notice, enough time to notice it, and a reason to care. Work in a film folder outside this skill. On a continuing film, open its continuity record, current cut, shot plan and approved assets first. Preserve the director's last decisions across revisions. On a new commission, ask or infer the audience, claim, viewing format and intended ending. A request to discuss or show stills stops at that stage.

## Work in this order

1. **Find the story.** Use [Story and narration](references/story.md). Verify claims against original sources. Write a small table of consecutive events with exact narration, what moves, what the viewer learns and the question that earns the next shot. Reserve time for looking and listening. Say the lines aloud. Agree the story at the level the director requested.
2. **Place the camera.** Use [Camera and space](references/camera.md). Show one continuous top-down movement sheet per new scene with a scale cue, setting, timed path, height, sightlines and final view. Get the director's approval before detailed drawing, set construction or animation. Where the director delegates approval, record who made the decision and why. Continue beats on that sheet. Revise a changed move there.
3. **Build and prove the artwork.** Use [Art and reusable assets](references/art.md) and [the production record](templates/production.md). Search the existing library first. Give every recognisable real subject its own photo, editable source, neutral model view and final linework from the shot camera. Register these even if later the subject appears in a different treatment. Test a model switch-off in the production renderer. Use the graphic evidence route for documents, maps and typography. Present the actual picture at phone size.
4. **Make the rough action legible.** Use [Motion and effects](references/motion.md). Test the most uncertain short passage with sound. Keep the camera, contacts, intervening objects and aftermath physically consistent. Check in real time as well as in stills. Resolve the first failure before multiplying it across scenes.
5. **Score the picture and direct the voice.** Use [Score, voice and captions](references/sound.md) at script timing, at recording and on the final mix. Spot real transients, reserve drop windows, edit the take and place captions after timing is stable. Carry beds across intended joins. Print dialogue, music and effects separately from the same origin.
6. **Judge the export.** Use [Review and handoff](references/review.md). The portable [tools](references/tools.md) can check the art evidence, assemble a new film, measure decoded tracks and export a report. Watch the encoded file with sound at normal speed and phone size, also in mono. Ask a fresh viewer about the clue and the consequence before sharing the cue sheet. Save open issues or the verified file hash with the cut.
7. **Leave it reusable.** Put approved editable assets, materials, sounds, parameters and licences in the component catalogue. Write down the concrete failure, the test that exposes it and what the new export proves. Link those back to the next shot's plan. Keep project-specific choices in the project.

For a new reference study or to reconcile an old one, use [Study a film](references/study.md). Find editable art, effects and audio in the [component library](references/components.md). Start with [setup](references/setup.md) and the [worked short](examples/the-gap/README.md) on a fresh machine. The software and example run independently of any earlier film.
