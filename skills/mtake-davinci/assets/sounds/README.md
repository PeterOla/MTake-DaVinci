# Original felt-piano cues

These are isolated, dialogue-free prints from the Halifax production's own synthesised felt keys. The stereo 48 kHz FLAC files retain the source samples losslessly. Use them as music, or cut an individual note into a cue. Original production material is released here under MIT.

| Sound | Length | Editorial use |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Early piano build](early-piano-build.flac) | 18.29 s | Hear the tension begin on the first clue and the spaces between the low notes change. Re-spot the cue to the new event. |
| [Shelter phrase](shelter-phrase.flac) | 3.51 s | A short warmer phrase at a human-scale turn. Leave space for the words. |
| [Dry felt key, D3](felt-key-d3.flac) | 3.00 s | A single pitched key with its own decay and short stereo return. Use it to audition a new rhythm. |

For another original timbre or a separate rising effect, [components.py](../../scripts/components.py) writes editable-duration synthesis to WAV. To bridge a picture cut, keep one room or environmental bed running underneath and audition the cut with the picture hidden. For a quiet ending, carry the source across the final held image and audition the receding tail at phone level. Record the licence and edit points when adding a new field recording.
