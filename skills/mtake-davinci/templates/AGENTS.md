# Film handoff

Use the installed **mtake-davinci** skill for filmmaking work in this project. Open its SKILL.md, then this project's production.md and project.json before resuming a scene. Check the current cut, decisions, camera approval, asset sources, sound clock, outstanding revision and the viewer's intended discovery.

At a new scene, show the single continuous camera sheet and record approval or an existing delegation before detailed art and animation. At a continuing scene use the agreed path. Inspect the photo, neutral model, production linework and source switch-off at the art handoff. Record the artist's decision with lock-art. When using the dedicated editor, its art and review commands check the registered assets against the actual encoded frame. Carry the approved score, voice, captions and continuity into each candidate. Document the exported file, screening observations and any open issue in production.md.

If the skill is being used as a manual rather than installed in the agent, its entry point is available in the [MTake DaVinci repository](https://github.com/PeterOla/MTake-DaVinci/tree/main/skills/mtake-davinci).
