# Build an independent reference study

Pick a legally accessible reference film. Record provenance, permission for private study versus redistribution, duration, resolution, frame rate or presentation timestamps, and a content fingerprint. Group by distinct cut before calculating cross-film statistics. A re-upload can carry the same picture and audio.

Watch once, normally, with sound. Write what the first-time viewer notices and wonders, when their understanding changes and what the last image leaves them doing. Then mark scene boundaries, camera changes, contacts, sound entrances, speech and changes in meaning. Use native frame numbers for fast events, and time ranges for holds. For variable-frame-rate sources read actual timestamps.

Build a reader someone can use without the film. For each meaningful beat record these observations:

| Channel | Write down |
| --- | --- |
| Picture | Subject count and priority, framing and position, silhouette, foreground and background, scale cue, face values, distinctive colours, line weights, texture, what is occluded and what just became visible. Sample a representative colour away from a compression edge. |
| Motion | Camera path against a fixed landmark, moving parts, pivot/contact, entry and exit, overlap, weight, change remaining in the next shot. Record the poses before, on and after a fast event. |
| Effects | Shape family, number and order of visible layers, warm core vs cooler foreground, which face is lit, depth, onset, maximum extent relative to frame and subject, disappearance. |
| Sound | Instrument family with confidence, onset/end, pitch trend or noisy broadband, level, attack/body/tail, stereo perspective and what is heard on a phone. Distinguish observations from naming a specific instrument or recording. |
| Performance and script | Audible words at the turning point, pauses, last syllable before an accent, first word after it, word count and usable speaking time. State timing uncertainty if stems are unavailable. |
| Viewer | What could be inferred without narration? What additional fact did the voice give? What is the reason for the next camera move? |

Collect stills around specific changes, with one wide that preserves scale. A contact sheet every two seconds can miss an entire flash. Pair the observations with timecoded landmarks and a short original reconstruction using a different subject. For sound use waveform, spectrogram and normal-speed listening together. Keep labels for observed, measured and inferred details.

At the end compare multiple studies: repeated choice, verified counterexample, editorial reason and a new subject that could benefit. Avoid promoting a single-film exception into a rule. Keep evidence access compatible with the source licence. Publish original written findings and worked examples when third-party pictures or recordings cannot travel with the toolkit.
