# Photo-grounded 3D linework

## Prove a recognisable subject

Find a real image of the subject and a real image of its surroundings when the setting matters. Prefer the exact place and period. A representative reference is acceptable when identified as such. Use the licence on the asset page; “free download” alone says little about redistribution. Save author, original URL, rights, changes and what features were adopted. Check the library before buying, downloading or drawing anything new.

Match camera height, horizon, principal planes and scale first. Derive an editable mesh from the photograph, load a suitably licensed 3D model, or commission one. Distinguish which method produced which asset. A hand-drawn caption, arrow, or abstract card is a graphic choice. For identifiable buildings, a vehicle, a person, a shoe or a handheld device, show the actual geometry and the real reference. For a portrait/gesture use a photograph or a properly posed human model. Inspect the bend at the wrist, thumb contact, finger overlap and whether a leg intersects the furniture. A sitting pose has to work both in the wide and when the audience sees the controller leave the hand.

Produce a board with the reference, neutral mesh and final linework from the shot camera. Keep production and neutral frames separate. For the visible subject, render once with it enabled and once with just it disabled. Keep the camera, lights, seed, background, effects and pose fixed. Check that the expected outline disappears exactly where it is used. Register foreground and background independently. A downloaded model on disk proves little about the pixels in the film. Link every later redrawing that carries the same identity back to that source. See [tools](tools.md) for a runnable check. Look at the board yourself even when the measurement passes.

## The drawn image

Save the inspected evidence with the **lock-art** command. Register the corresponding production frame in the scene's shot list. Assembly and review check the visible encoded result against that frame. [Scene, subject and checkpoint examples](tools.md#register-the-source-before-the-cut-exists)

- Block large masses in value first. In the dark treatment, distinguish the front, side, roof and ground at phone size. Keep large faces charcoal; a few warm-lit edges describe the form. Broad cream is useful as a purposeful event, document or light source.
- Use a heavier outer silhouette, selective middle-weight seams at changes of plane and finer detail for depth. On a 1080-wide working canvas, try 3–5 pixels for the hero contour and 1–2 for interior marks, then decide from the downsampled export. A distant silhouette can lose surface detail and still carry the correct outline. These widths are starting values.
- Let a cornice, window return, tyre wall, bevel or cuff turn the corner. Parallel stripes drawn across a flat face rarely describe its depth. Remove lines first from the least important plane. Compare width and value using frames at the same output scale.
- Light paper scenes use dark ink silhouettes, fine grey boundaries, faint grain and white space around evidence. Match one object or edge across the dark-to-light transition. For maps use genuine geometry and terrain when the relief tells the story. Assign accent colours to meaning, keep fill and labels distinct, and inspect the whole frame for accidental figures or textures.
- A caption is a separate information layer. A reasonable inherited starting treatment uses a centred warm serif in a band near 72% of frame height, usually one or two lines; reserve a muted red for a meaningful word. The local starting colours for copy and accent were #f7ecd6 and #d88672. Adapt the band to a platform's controls and to anything the audience needs to see.

## Assets that survive the next project

Keep editable source, provenance, licence, dependencies, scale, pivot, front direction, neutral view, phone preview and parameter defaults with each library entry. Instantiate a lamp, window, tile, bed or paper card from one source throughout a scene. Save reusable effects with independent duration, apparent size, palette and depth. Store dry audio with its tail and named type. Prove reuse in a different small scene or camera. A cropped study frame belongs to comparison evidence with its rights information; make original production artwork.
