# Review the exported film

1. **Resume from the decision record.** Name the current cut, approved palette, font, voice, score, hero sources and camera sheet. At a revision, compare these to the last accepted release. Run the full relevant review again on the candidate, including unchanged-looking passages around a join.
2. **Compare pictures that serve the same purpose.** Hold three matched poses at native resolution and phone size. Compare foreground scale, line hierarchy, face separation, focal object count, camera continuity, onset and aftermath. A close-up can accidentally make the smallest thing look largest. Compare the subject-to-frame ratio and behaviour in motion. If using a private study, keep it in the review room.
3. **Check the evidence against the picture.** Render neutral, on and off from one locked camera and seed for every modelled hero subject. A plausible source file that doesn't alter the expected part of the production frame is a failed handoff. Keep the crop, changed fraction and thumbnail board. For a map, check boundary, relief, labels and viewpoint against the named dataset and visual treatment. For retained footage or a document, check the original file, its rights, crop and readability.
4. **Check the sound.** Use the same origin for the stems, listen to each alone, then play the exported stereo and phone mono. The mix report includes effect onsets, continuing effects, per-window RMS and a comparison with the printed stems. Timing anchors compare picture and sound. Also listen for a weak quiet buildup or a harsh join even when levels pass.
5. **Run negative controls.** On a disposable copy remove the named model, shift the expected change outside the region, mute a music print, move a chosen impact by a frame, and let a spoken word overlap a protected beat. The affected checks should fail. Restore the accepted inputs, rerun review and record the candidate fingerprint. [Tool behaviour and limitations](tools.md)
6. **Test the story blind.** Give a new viewer only the exported cut. Ask what they noticed first, what caused the outcome, what the camera followed and when the music took over. Note their answer verbatim with the time. An agent who can inspect samples but cannot listen or watch continuously should specify that limitation. Keep the technical review and the viewer's actual response in separate fields.
7. **Hand over one clearly named master.** State running time, aspect ratio and which cut was reviewed. Include captions, credits and applicable stems or editable assets. Save the specific failure and the test that would have caught it before drawing or recording. Confirm another project can open each newly catalogued component without your workstation's absolute paths.
